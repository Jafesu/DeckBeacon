## Result
- **PASS (main question):** Steam's Gaming Mode suspend goes through logind. The agent's delay inhibitor gave it time to announce `device_suspending`, and HA recorded "suspended" before the device went offline, in **both** modes. Gaming/Desktop mode detection is correct on this unit.
- **Issues found and fixed** (agent and integration, after this test):
  1. Connection stayed "local" for ~45 s after the device slept → the agent now closes the session itself right after HA acks the announcement, and waits for resume (2 min fallback) before reconnecting.
  2. Duplicate resume → the clock-jump heuristic is now used only when logind is unavailable.
  3. A reload or HA restart lost an announced "suspended" → power state is now persisted per device.
- Re-test after the fixes: pending (see the next evidence entry).

Power-off → `off` once the drop happens. Reboot → `rebooting` (from logind metadata) until the device returns. "Last boot" is pinned per boot_id. Lifecycle steps are logged.
